Limitations

  • One synthetic main fixture plus a naming-shift holdout. Not a benchmark and not a generalisation claim.
  • No live tenant, no customer code, no Cloud API generation, and no deployment.
  • Findings are pattern-based and advisory. Official Cloud Assurance was not run.
  • Impact ranges are heuristic planning ranges, not quotes or measured productivity.
  • Demo metrics are in-sample on the development slice; the naming-shift holdout is pre-computed and never imported.
  • M3 audits excerpt support at the source, not semantic entailment. M6 uses three positives and three hard negatives per rule; intervals remain wide at this n.
  • No human inter-annotator study was run; the reference target and concordance process are labelled AI-assisted.

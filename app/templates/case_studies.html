What the case studies prove

evidence-bound, not a certification

Code-level blockers are identifiable

Direct JDBC, dynamic SQL, legacy message transports, servlets, removed Java APIs, static tenant state, weak crypto, hard-coded secrets, APD naming, and DB-coupled GUnit tests produce line-located findings with stable fingerprints.

Refactors are reviewable artifacts

The Architect emits target drafts, cloud configuration, unified diffs, and semantic-review notes. Nothing is deployed and every patch stays pending.

Gates survive platform movement

A mid-migration release upgrade invalidates the previous green signal, identifies affected patches, and offers revalidate, rollback, or defer choices.

Findings reach developer pipelines

SARIF 2.1.0 carries rule metadata, line locations, evidence fingerprints, remediation, and claim strength without embedding source excerpts or secrets.
